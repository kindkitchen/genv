## DEPLOY.md
Wrote DEPLOY.md documenting the candidate-tag release flow that breaks the
self-invocation deadlock (a tag must exist before its e2e can run, but main
must not advance until green):
1 branch/change, 2 `just bump`, 3 author the version's e2e (trigger on both
`vX.Y.Z` and `vX.Y.Z-candidate`, pin `uses:@vX.Y.Z-candidate`), 4 push/force-move
the candidate tag and loop fixes, 5 promote `uses:` -> `@vX.Y.Z`, 6 push the
immutable final tag (final gate), 7 squash-merge to main, 8 reset dev to main +
delete candidate tag.
Referenced it from AGENTS.md under a new `release:` section.

to reconcile per DEPLOY.md notes before finalizing v1.0.1.
